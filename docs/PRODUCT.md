# Product

Author presentations and present them interactively. Slides and content editing are the default workflow; motion is an optional authoring layer. Preserve continuous visual teaching stories through: stable actors across scenes, logical slides with teaching steps, editable motion and independent camera. The first local slice makes the PACKET reference editable and durable.

Delivered surfaces: local presentation library/search, new blank or PACKET project, JSON import; Studio canvas/layers/inspector/actors/history/timeline; draft autosave; immutable revision and publish; read-only deep-linked viewer.

No login, workspace membership, archive/delete, collaboration, categories, permission administration, arbitrary domain-component SDK, or full export/import platform yet. These stay documented rather than becoming nonfunctional controls.
